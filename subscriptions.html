Subscriptions & Purchases

KekeColour offers free colouring content alongside optional paid ways to explore more artwork.

Free Colouring

Enjoy the free colouring pages available in KekeColour without purchasing Premium.

KekeColour Premium

Premium is a subscription that unlocks eligible collections and unlimited image uploads while it is active.

The price and billing period are shown on the Membership screen, and the store shows its renewal terms and any introductory offer before you confirm.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in accordance with the relevant app-store terms.

Cancelling generally stops future renewals; access normally continues until the end of the paid subscription period, subject to the store’s terms.

Buy Once

Some colouring books can be bought with a one-time purchase.

Buy Once gives you permanent ownership of that book’s pages without starting a recurring subscription. Books you own appear in My Books.

The price is shown on the book’s page before you buy.

Restore Purchases

If you bought Premium or a book before, sign in to the same KekeColour account and use Restore purchases.

Restoring depends on the store account used for the purchase and your purchase entitlement.

Managing Subscriptions

Subscriptions bought through Apple are managed in your Apple account’s subscription settings. Subscriptions bought through Google Play are managed in Google Play.

Deleting KekeColour or deleting your KekeColour account does not automatically cancel an active store subscription.

Refunds

Refunds may be requested through the relevant app store, or contact us for help.

Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.
