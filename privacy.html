Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to KekeColour, a creative colouring application operated by Kyara Costelloe under the Keke & Co brand in Australia.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use KekeColour.

This Privacy Policy explains our information-handling practices, including how colouring progress is stored, how optional accounts work, and how to contact us about privacy matters.

Contact: kekecolour@gmail.com

2. Using KekeColour Without an Account

You can use KekeColour’s available guest features without creating an account.

When you colour as a guest, your colouring progress is saved locally on your device and is not synchronised to an account.

Guest colouring progress may be lost if you uninstall the app, clear its data, change devices or lose access to your device.

Even without an account, the app connects to our backend to load colouring content, and our purchase provider, RevenueCat, assigns the app an anonymous identifier so purchases can be managed. As with any internet service, our service providers receive technical information such as your IP address when the app connects to them.

3. Optional Accounts

You may choose to create or sign in to a KekeColour account using an email address and password, with Apple (on iPhone and iPad) or with Google.

Depending on the sign-in method, we process your email address, an account identifier and authentication information. If you sign in with Google, Google shares basic account details, such as your name, email address and profile picture, with our authentication provider. If you sign in with Apple, Apple shares an account identifier and the email address you choose, which may be a private relay address if you use Hide My Email. We don’t store the name Apple may share.

We use account information to authenticate users, provide account-based features and associate cloud-synchronised colouring progress with the correct account.

The display name, bio and pictures you add on your Profile are stored only on your device.

We do not require users to create an account to access the guest colouring features.

4. Colouring Progress and Cloud Synchronisation

Guest colouring progress is stored locally on the device.

When you are signed in to a KekeColour account, the app synchronises colouring progress between compatible devices signed in to that account.

This involves storing your coloured pages, the original line art they were coloured on, saved versions, page and collection titles, and related technical details such as revision numbers and save times, in private cloud storage linked to your account.

Artwork you coloured as a guest is not uploaded unless you choose to add it to your account.

Cloud synchronisation depends on connectivity, successful authentication and the availability of our services. Please don’t assume that all locally stored content has been synchronised until the app confirms that it has been saved.

5. Uploaded Images and Artwork

KekeColour allows you to import your own images for personal colouring.

Imported images are stored on your device. If you are signed in and colour an imported image, the original image and your coloured page are saved to your private cloud storage, like other artwork, so they can synchronise between your devices. They are removed from cloud storage when your account is deleted.

Imported images may contain personal information, including photographs or other identifying details. Please avoid uploading sensitive information unless necessary.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

KekeColour offers optional paid features, including Premium subscriptions and individual colouring book purchases.

Payments are handled through the relevant app-store payment system. RevenueCat is used to help manage and verify purchase entitlements. When you are signed in, your KekeColour account identifier is used as your RevenueCat customer identifier so purchases can be linked to your account.

We process purchase identifiers, product information, the store and transaction identifier, purchase and expiry dates, subscription status and entitlement records needed to provide purchased content and restore eligible purchases.

We do not collect or store payment card details through the KekeColour app.

The relevant app store and RevenueCat have their own privacy policies, which you may review separately.

7. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain KekeColour.
  • Authenticate optional accounts.
  • Save and synchronise colouring progress for signed-in users.
  • Provide access to purchased content.
  • Restore eligible purchases.
  • Respond to support and privacy enquiries.
  • Protect the security and reliability of the service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

KekeColour does not currently include advertising, third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools, and does not send push notifications. If this changes, we will update this policy.

8. Third-Party Services

KekeColour uses the following third-party services to provide certain functionality:

  • Supabase: backend infrastructure, authentication and cloud data services.
  • RevenueCat: subscription, purchase-entitlement and related purchase-management functionality.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, if you choose to sign in with Apple.
  • Google: sign-in, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: payment processing, purchase management and platform services.

Some of these providers are based outside Australia, so your information may be stored or processed overseas.

9. Children’s Privacy

KekeColour is an all-ages creative application.

Children can use available guest colouring features without creating an account.

We aim to minimise unnecessary collection of personal information, particularly from younger users.

Parents and guardians should supervise account creation, purchases and the use of personal images where appropriate.

Signing in with a third-party account does not, by itself, establish that any required parental consent has been obtained.

Parents and guardians may contact kekecolour@gmail.com with concerns about a child’s personal information.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through KekeColour. Cloud artwork is kept in private storage that only your signed-in account can access.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We will review and respond to identified security issues as appropriate.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law.

Local colouring progress remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app.

Your account, cloud artwork, saved versions and account-linked access are kept while your account exists and are deleted when you delete your account. Records of store transactions, such as the product, transaction identifier and date, may be kept after deletion, for example for accounting, refunds and fraud prevention.

12. Access, Correction and Deletion

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.

Signed-in users can request account deletion in the app from Profile & settings → Delete account, or by emailing us.

Deleting an account is different from cancelling an app-store subscription. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel recurring payments managed by Apple or another store.

For enquiries, contact kekecolour@gmail.com.

13. Privacy Questions and Complaints

If you have questions or concerns about how your information is handled, please email kekecolour@gmail.com.

We will review privacy enquiries and complaints and respond within a reasonable period.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may also have the right to raise a complaint with a relevant privacy regulator.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in KekeColour, its services or applicable legal requirements.

The updated policy will display its most recent revision date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent before making material changes to how information is handled.
