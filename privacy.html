Privacy policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Keke Colour is a colouring application offered by Keke & Co. This page explains the kinds of information that may be processed when you use the app and how to contact us.

Information used to provide the app

Depending on how you use Keke Colour, the app may process account details such as an email address, saved colouring progress, uploaded images, and information needed to determine access to Premium subscriptions or purchased books.

Service providers

Keke Colour uses Supabase for app services including account, database and storage functionality, and RevenueCat to help manage purchase entitlements. Apple processes purchases made through the App Store. Each provider may process information as needed to deliver its services.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the app, save or sync colouring activity, provide access to purchased content, respond to support requests, and protect the service.

Uploaded artwork

Images you choose to upload are used to provide the app's colouring functionality. Please avoid uploading sensitive personal information you do not want stored or processed.

Retention and deletion

We retain information as needed to provide the service and meet applicable obligations. You can request account and personal data deletion by following our account deletion instructions. Some transaction records may need to be retained where required by law.

Children and families

Keke Colour offers colouring content that may appeal to families. Parents and guardians can contact us with privacy questions concerning a child's use of the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, email kekecolour@gmail.com.

Publication review required: This policy must be checked against the final app's actual data collection, analytics, permissions, retention, international transfers and child-related practices before being used for App Store submission.
