Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Keke Colour is a colouring app provided by Keke & Co. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the app.

Accounts and guest use

You can use free colouring features as a guest. Guest colouring work is saved on your device. If you create or sign into an account, we process your account identifier and details supplied for sign-in, such as an email address. Signed-in users can use cloud storage and syncing for their artwork.

Artwork and uploads

We process colouring progress, saved artwork and images you choose to upload to provide colouring, saving and syncing features. Uploaded files and cloud artwork may be stored using our hosting provider. Please avoid uploading private or sensitive material unless you are comfortable with it being processed for these features.

Purchases and access

Apple processes App Store payments. Keke Colour uses RevenueCat and our app backend to recognise subscriptions, one-time book purchases, purchase restoration and access rights. Purchase and entitlement records can be associated with your signed-in account identifier. We do not receive your full payment-card details from Apple.

Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication, databases and file storage, RevenueCat for purchase management, and Apple for App Store payments and related account services. Information may be processed in countries outside Australia, depending on the provider and service.

Why we use information

We use information to provide the app, manage accounts, save and sync artwork, make purchased content available, respond to support requests and maintain service security. We do not claim that purchase information or cloud artwork is anonymous when it is linked to an account.

Retention and deletion

Guest artwork remains on your device until you remove it or the app's local data. Account holders can use the in-app account deletion option, where available, or contact us for assistance. Deletion of account-associated data may be subject to limited legal, fraud-prevention or transaction-record retention obligations. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Children and families

Some Keke Colour content may appeal to children and families. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of accounts, uploads and purchases. Contact us if you have a privacy concern relating to a child.
