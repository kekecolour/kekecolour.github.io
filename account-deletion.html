Delete Your KekeColour Account

We’re sorry to see you go.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your account and the personal data KekeColour holds for it:

  • Your sign-in account and authentication records. If you used Sign in with Apple, KekeColour’s access to your Apple ID is also revoked.
  • Your cloud artwork, saved versions and the original images synced with them, including imported images.
  • Premium, Buy Once and other access linked to your account.

Records of store transactions, such as the product, transaction identifier and date, may be kept where needed, for example for accounting, refunds and fraud prevention. They are no longer linked to your account.

Deletion starts as soon as you confirm. If it can’t finish straight away, cloud saving stays paused for your account until it completes.

Important things to know

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • You will lose access to cloud-synchronised artwork and account-linked services.
  • Deleting an account does not cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it in your store account first.
  • Purchases and restoration rights remain subject to the applicable store rules and consumer law.
  • Artwork saved on your device is not deleted. Remove it by deleting those pages or uninstalling the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Profile & settings.
  2. Select Delete account.
  3. Review the information shown.
  4. Confirm that you want to proceed.
  5. If asked, enter your password or confirm with Apple or Google to confirm it’s you.

If you cannot access the app or your account, contact kekecolour@gmail.com.

Request deletion by email

Email kekecolour@gmail.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request” from the email address linked to your account.

We may need to verify that you are authorised to request deletion before processing the request.
