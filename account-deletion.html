Account deletion

To request deletion of your Keke Colour account and associated personal data, contact kekecolour@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject “Keke Colour Account Deletion”.

We may need to verify that you own the account before processing your request. We'll explain what data will be deleted and whether any records must be retained for legal or transaction-related reasons.

Important: Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Before App Store submission, we'll also verify that account deletion is available within the app wherever Apple requires it.
