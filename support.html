Help & Support

We’re here to make your colouring experience as cosy and easy as possible.

Can I use KekeColour without an account?

Yes! You can enjoy available guest colouring features without creating an account.

Where is my colouring saved?

Guest colouring progress is saved locally on the device.

Signed-in users can synchronise colouring progress across devices using the same KekeColour account.

Will I lose my colouring if I uninstall the app?

Guest colouring progress may be lost when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

Signed-in users should make sure their progress has synchronised before changing devices or deleting local data.

How do I access my colouring on another device?

Sign in to the same KekeColour account on the other device and allow the app to synchronise your saved progress.

How do I use Smart Colouring?

In the colouring editor, choose a drawing tool, open its settings and turn on Stay Inside the Lines and Smart Colouring.

Smart Colouring tries to keep your marker within the main outlines of an object while letting colour pass underneath finer decorative lines. Results may vary depending on the illustration.

Can I colour freely?

Yes. Turn off Stay Inside the Lines to colour freely. Outlines always stay on top.

Can I upload my own images?

Yes. Use Upload an image to import image files from your device for personal colouring. Without Premium you can upload up to 5 images; Premium removes the limit.

What is Premium?

Premium is an optional subscription that unlocks eligible collections and unlimited uploads while it is active.

What does Buy Once mean?

Buy Once is a one-time purchase of a specific colouring book. You own it permanently, with no ongoing subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in to the same KekeColour account and use Restore purchases (Profile & settings → Subscriptions & purchases). If your purchase isn’t restored, contact us.

How do I cancel Premium?

Manage or cancel your subscription through the app store used for the purchase. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile & settings and select Delete account, then follow the confirmation steps. If you can’t access your account, contact kekecolour@gmail.com.
