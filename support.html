Saved colouring and uploads

Guests save artwork on their device. Signed-in users can use supported cloud saving and syncing. If artwork is missing, tell us your device, whether you were signed in, and what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Premium, Buy Once and restoration

Premium gives access to eligible books while your subscription is active. Buy Once is a separate purchase for an eligible book. If access is missing, check your Apple account and try Restore Purchases in the app. Contact us if it still doesn't appear.

Subscriptions and refunds

Manage or cancel Apple subscriptions through your Apple account settings. For App Store refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
