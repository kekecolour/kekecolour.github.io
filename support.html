Colouring and saved artwork

If a page isn't loading or your colouring progress isn't appearing, tell us which device you're using, whether you're signed in, and what happened. Please don't email passwords or payment card information.

Premium and Buy Once

If a purchase isn't showing, check that you're signed into the correct Apple account and use the Restore Purchases option in Keke Colour. If you're still having trouble, email us with the book name and a description of the issue.

Billing and refunds

Purchases made through Apple's App Store are billed by Apple. For App Store refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Account and privacy

For privacy questions or help deleting your account, visit our account deletion page or email us.
